# Anytime Coach — an AI coach your members pay for, that keeps them coming back

*A 1-page proposal for the Kelowna / Okanagan ownership group (11 locations)*

---

## The problem you already pay for

Every gym's real cost isn't acquisition — it's **churn**. Industry attrition runs
30–50% a year, and it's overwhelmingly members who quietly **stop showing up**,
then cancel. By the time a membership cancels, the member is long gone. You have
the one signal that predicts it — **key-fob check-ins** — but nobody's watching
it across 11 sites, and nobody has the hours to personally re-engage a member
who's slipping.

## The product

**Anytime Coach** — a white-label app you brand as your own and sell to members
as a **$15–25/month add-on**. Two things run inside it:

1. **An AI personal coach** in every member's pocket, 24/7. It builds a real
   training program from their goals and **the equipment actually in their home
   gym**, swaps exercises when a machine's busy, and answers questions — for a
   fraction of what a human PT session costs.
2. **An accountability engine** that watches the check-in feed, scores who's
   drifting, and sends warm, personal nudges to win them back **before** they
   cancel. Anyone who reports pain is automatically handed to a human trainer.

You see it all on an **owner dashboard**: your whole roster ranked by churn
risk, who got nudged, and who needs a human follow-up.

## Why it makes you money

| Lever | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Add-on revenue** | High-margin. ~1,650 members × $19/mo ≈ **$31K/month** in new recurring revenue, at near-zero staff cost. |
| **Retention** | Catching a lapsing member at "medium risk" is far cheaper than re-selling a cancelled one. A few points of churn across 11 sites dwarfs the add-on revenue. |
| **PT funnel** | The AI routes plateaued members to your **paid** human trainers — it feeds your higher-margin services, it doesn't replace them. |

Your cost to us is a flat per-location fee plus a small per-active-member fee —
a fraction of what you collect from members. **You keep the spread.**

## Why this isn't just another fitness chatbot

- **It knows *your* gyms.** Equipment, classes, and policies are loaded per
  location, so it never prescribes a machine a site doesn't have.
- **It's safe and compliant by design.** Member health data is scrubbed before
  it ever reaches the AI, with Canadian (PIPEDA / BC PIPA) data residency — the
  part your lawyer will ask about. It gives fitness coaching, never medical
  advice, and escalates any injury to your staff.
- **It's built, not a slide.** A working demo runs today: program generation,
  member chat, the risk dashboard, and the re-engagement sweep.

## What we'd need from you to pilot

1. **Access to the check-in feed** — the export from your club-management /
   key-fob system. This is the one dependency; the accountability engine is only
   as good as the attendance signal. (A CSV export is enough to start.)
2. **One pilot location.** We seed it, run for 60–90 days, and measure retention
   against a comparable site.
3. A decision on member price point and whether you want the AI→human-PT upsell.

## Proposed next step

A 30-minute walkthrough of the live demo (owner dashboard + member app), then a
single-location pilot. If the retention numbers hold at one gym, we roll out to
all 11.
