Land & Responsibility

Out here, we're all guests.

We operate on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territories of the Sḵwx̱wú7mesh (Squamish) Nation, whose relationship with these mountains, forests, and waters stretches back thousands of years.

We feel incredibly fortunate to explore these trails, and we carry that responsibility with care. Every ride is approached with respect for the land: staying on designated routes, treading lightly, and leaving each place better than we found it.

We're committed to safe riding, thoughtful stewardship, and encouraging every guest to appreciate the land not just for what it offers, but for everything it represents.

Out here, we're all guests. And we're grateful for every minute we get to spend in these mountains.

We honour the knowledge keepers and community members who continue their relationship with these lands today.
