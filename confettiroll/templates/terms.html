The deal

ConfettiRoll gives your event a private shared photo album. Hosts create events; guests add photos with the event password. Every event starts with a free week; hosts keep an album permanently by purchasing a package or applying an event credit. Beta pricing is honored for the life of an unlocked album.

Your content, your responsibility

You keep ownership of everything you upload, and you give us only the limited permission needed to store and display it inside your private gallery. Please only upload photos you have the right to share, and nothing unlawful or harmful. Hosts can remove any photo from their album; we can remove content that breaks these rules.

Payments & printed books

Payments are processed by Stripe. Packages are one-time, per-event purchases. Printed keepsake books are priced by cover and length as shown at checkout, ship to one address per order, and the PDF edition of your book is always free. If a printed book arrives damaged, email us and we'll make it right.

The service

We work hard to keep ConfettiRoll fast and available, especially on event days, but during the beta the service is provided as-is. Your photos remain downloadable at all times, and deleting your event removes them from our servers permanently.

Questions

Anything unclear? Email hello@confettiroll.com — a human reads everything.
