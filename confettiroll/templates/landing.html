Questions, answered

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan the QR code (or type the address), enter the event password once, and upload straight from their phone's browser — iPhone HEIC photos and videos included.

Who can see our photos?

Only people with your event password. Galleries are private by default, every photo request is checked, and you can remove anything as the host.

What sizes of events does it handle?

Anything from a dinner party to a 300-guest wedding. Photos upload at full quality up to 30 MB each, videos up to 200 MB.

How long do we keep the gallery?

12 months on Celebration and 24 on Heirloom — and you can download everything, including the keepsake book PDF, at any time.

I run a venue — how does white-label work?

You get your own branded page (your logo, colors, and photos, with an AI brand agent that writes the copy) on your own domain. Every event you host gets a gallery carrying your brand, plus the live photo wall for your TVs. Details here.
