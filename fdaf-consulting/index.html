FAQ

Things people ask before booking.

What areas do you serve?

West Kelowna is home base. I regularly travel to Kelowna, Peachland, Lake Country and surrounding Central Okanagan neighbourhoods. Outside that radius, a small travel fee may apply — just ask.

Do I need any equipment or space?

Nope. I bring mats, bands, dumbbells, sliders and whatever else the workout calls for. As long as you have roughly a living-room-sized space, we're good. Garages, decks and backyards are great too.

What's the minimum and maximum group size?

Couples sessions are designed for two. Small-group sessions run best with 3–6 people. Beyond that, the workout starts to feel more like a class than coaching — reach out and we can plan something custom.

I'm a total beginner. Will I be embarrassed?

Not a chance. Most of my clients haven't trained in years — or ever. Everything is scaled to whoever is in the room, and we go at the pace your body needs. The point is to feel better, not to suffer.

What about injuries or pregnancy?

I work with both regularly. I'll ask about your history during the consult and adapt programming accordingly. For complex medical situations, I'll always coordinate with your physician or physiotherapist.

How do I pay, and what's your cancellation policy?

E-transfer or credit card. 24-hour cancellation notice is appreciated; same-day cancellations are charged at 50%.
